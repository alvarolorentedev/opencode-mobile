- Exposed services can now claim a dedicated HTTPS port, so apps that emit absolute URLs work from the port root and not only a sub-path.
- Each service shows a full shareable link (for example `https://host:2053/` or `:8443/app`), and port conflicts are caught before saving.
- Older Cloud Link connectors keep the legacy `:8443` paths with a clear compatibility hint.
- Returning to a chat after switching apps or unlocking the phone keeps your place, instead of jumping back to the top of the conversation.
